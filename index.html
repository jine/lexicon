Journey to the Stars

Space exploration has always captivated the human imagination. From the first steps on the Moon to the robotic rovers on Mars, we continue to push the boundaries of what is possible.

The Solar System

Our cosmic neighborhood:

  • The Sun: The star at the center of our Solar System. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma, heated to incandescence by nuclear fusion reactions in its core.
  • Mercury: The smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest to the Sun. Its orbit around the Sun takes 87.97 Earth days, the shortest of all the Sun's planets.
  • Venus: The second planet from the Sun. It has a dense atmosphere consisting mostly of carbon dioxide, trapping heat and making it the hottest planet.
  • Earth: Our home, the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered with water.
  • Mars: The fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet. It is often referred to as the "Red Planet" due to the effect of the iron oxide prevalent on its surface.
  • Jupiter: The fifth planet from the Sun and the largest in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a mass one-thousandth that of the Sun, but two-and-a-half times that of all the other planets combined.

Historic Milestones

  1. 1957 - Sputnik 1: The Soviet Union launches the first artificial satellite into orbit, marking the beginning of the Space Age.
  2. 1961 - Yuri Gagarin: Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human to journey into outer space, completing one orbit of Earth.
  3. 1969 - Apollo 11: NASA's Apollo 11 mission lands the first humans on the Moon. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walk on the lunar surface.
  4. 1977 - Voyager Probes: Voyager 1 and 2 are launched to explore the outer planets. They are now the farthest human-made objects from Earth.
  5. 1990 - Hubble Space Telescope: Launched into low Earth orbit, Hubble has provided some of the most detailed images of the universe ever recorded.
  6. 2020 - Mars Perseverance Rover: NASA lands the Perseverance rover on Mars to seek signs of ancient life and collect samples for possible return to Earth.
